🏗️ Dilts Organizational Structure

Living Digital Representation of Individuals & Organizations

L6
MISSION
FOR WHAT - Why We Exist
Extreme Autonomy
Mission statements, vision, impact goals, ultimate purpose. Can question existence itself.
Examples: "Accelerate scientific discovery globally" | "Make education accessible to all" | "Solve climate crisis through technology"
L5
IDENTITY
WHO - What Defines Us
High Autonomy
Brand identity, core values, culture, positioning. Can redefine what we are.
Examples: "The trusted partner for innovation" | "Radical transparency, user-first" | "Premium quality, sustainable approach"
L4
BELIEFS
WHY - Our Guiding Principles
Moderate Autonomy
Principles, assumptions (validated/invalidated), mental models. Can choose approaches. Bidirectional flow!
Examples: "Quality over speed" (principle, conf: 0.95) | "Remote-first culture works" (validated, conf: 0.90) | "Market prefers feature X" (needs validation, conf: 0.55)
L3
CAPABILITIES
WHAT - What We Can Deliver
Low Autonomy
Services, products, competencies, resources. Can select methods.
Examples: "Enterprise consulting services" | "SaaS platform with API" | "AI/ML technical expertise" | "Global support infrastructure"
L2
BEHAVIORS
HOW - How We Operate
Very Low Autonomy
Strategic (APPROACH): Measured initiatives to serve market through identity. Tactical (RESPONSE): Market feedback informing strategic refinement. Bidirectional!
Examples: "Quarterly planning process" | "Customer feedback loops" | "Performance metrics review"
L1
ENVIRONMENT
WHERE/WHEN - Context We Operate In
Zero Autonomy
Target markets, stakeholders, constraints. Cannot change context, only adapt.
Examples: "Enterprise B2B in North America" | "Regulatory: GDPR, SOC2 compliance" | "Competitive: 5 major players" | "Economic: Budget constraints"

🔄 Strategy (Approach) ↔ Tactics (Response)
STRATEGY = APPROACH (Top-down initiative)
Measured initiatives designed to serve the market through our identity.
Mission → Identity → Beliefs → Capabilities → Behaviors → Environment
↓ We act upon the market
ENVIRONMENT RESPONDS
Market gives feedback to our strategic actions and observations.
↑ Market informs us
TACTICS = RESPONSE (Bottom-up feedback)
Measured responses to market feedback that inform strategic refinement.
Environment → Behaviors → Capabilities → Beliefs → Identity → Mission
📊 Confidence Tracking
Every dimension has a confidence score (0.0-1.0). Track uncertainty, update based on evidence, make decisions appropriate to confidence level.
🔬 Belief Validation
Assumptions move through lifecycle: created → validated/invalidated. Evidence-based organizational learning with full audit trail.
🌊 Bidirectional Flow
L4 (Beliefs) and L2 (Behaviors) flow both ways. Strategy informs tactics, reality validates assumptions. Feedback loops built-in.
📜 Git Integration
Version control for consciousness. Dimensional commits (WHY + HOW required), queryable history, full provenance at any point in time.
🔍 AI-Queryable

🎯 Full Traceability
Every action traces to mission. Ask "why?" at any level, get complete chain. No more "I don't know why we do this."
